Privacy Policy — ScreenBee 🐝

Version 2.0.4 · Last updated: September 2026

In short: your recordings never leave your computer. ScreenBee runs no servers, no analytics, and collects no personal data whatsoever. The only thing it keeps track of is how many minutes you have recorded today, so it can apply the free plan's limit — a number and a date, stored on your own machine.

1. What ScreenBee stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWhy
Video & audio you record In browser memory while recording, then written as a .webm file (or .mp4 with PRO) to your Downloads folder This is the product. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Preferences (language, recording mode, quality, format, mic on/off) chrome.storage.local on this device So the extension opens the way you left it
Free-plan usage counter — today's date and the number of minutes recorded today chrome.storage.local on this device To apply the free plan's daily limit. It is a single number and a date, nothing about what you recorded. It resets each day and never leaves your computer.

About Chrome Sync: anything written to chrome.storage.sync is synchronised by Google across Chrome profiles you are signed into. That is a Chrome feature; ScreenBee has no access to Google's copy.

2. Network connections

This version of ScreenBee makes no outbound network requests at all. It is a free edition with no paid tier, no licence activation and no account.

There is no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking script, and no remotely hosted font or code. Your recordings are never transmitted. If a paid tier is introduced in a future version, activating a licence key would contact the payment provider to verify it, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

3. Browser permissions and why each is needed

  • tabCapture — captures video and audio of the browser tab you chose to record.
  • Screen sharing (Full Screen mode) — uses the browser's own getDisplayMedia picker, which needs no extension permission at all. Chrome controls the picker and your consent; ScreenBee only receives the stream you approved.
  • activeTab + scripting — draws the countdown, the floating control bar and the webcam bubble on the page being recorded.
  • Camera & microphone (requested by the browser at the moment of use) — captures your webcam bubble and narration. Streams are processed in memory and released as soon as recording ends.
  • offscreen — runs MediaRecorder in a hidden document, as Manifest V3 requires.
  • storage — saves the preferences and the free-plan usage counter listed in section 1.
  • downloads — writes the finished .webm (or .mp4) file to your Downloads folder.
  • Access to all websites (optional, off by default) — you may grant this so the control bar survives when you navigate to another page mid-recording. ScreenBee never reads or transmits page content, and if you decline, recording still works normally.

4. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share user data with third parties. No data reaches us in the first place — this version sends nothing anywhere.

5. Retention and deletion

Recording data is discarded from memory once the file is written. The free-plan usage counter resets by itself every day. To erase everything else, remove the extension at chrome://extensions. Uninstalling clears everything ScreenBee stored on your device.

6. Children's privacy

ScreenBee is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data — in fact we collect no personal data from any user, of any age.

7. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published here with a new date and version number before they take effect.

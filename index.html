Questions

Do my recordings get uploaded anywhere?

No. Video and audio are processed in your browser's memory and written directly to your disk. ScreenBee runs no servers of any kind.

Why is the free version this generous?

Because a recorder that stamps a watermark on your work or cuts you off after five minutes isn't worth installing. PRO exists for people who need MP4 files for editing and publishing.

What's wrong with WebM?

Nothing, if you only watch it in a browser. But many video editors, presentation tools and platforms won't accept .webm. MP4 plays everywhere, which is why it's the paid feature.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The free version never makes a network request. Only PRO licence activation talks to the payment provider.

Can it record Chrome's own settings pages?

Not in Tab mode — Chrome forbids extensions from touching its internal pages. Use Full Screen mode for those.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers: Edge, Brave, Opera, Cốc Cốc. Chrome 116 or newer.
